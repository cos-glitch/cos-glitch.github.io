About Me:

I'm newly graduated from University College Dublin where I achieved my MSc in AI for medicine and medical research. The master focuses on learning ways to utilise AI/ML to improve medicine and medical research, with a hevay emphasis on cancer. My thesis, "Machine Learning Based Biomarker Discovery for Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma Using Serum Metabolites", analysed serum metabolites found from performing untargeted metabolomics using LC/MS on serum from known pancreatic cancer patients. I built an ML pipeline in Python, from data loading and cleaning through model training and testing to final model results. An excel list of high impact metabolites were extracted and used for pathway analysis. This project highlights my proficiency in applying ML to real-world healthcare challenges, a field I'm deeply passionate about.

My background is a BSc in Computer Science from Swansea University where I built a strong foundation in logic, ML, and software development. In my final year of my bachelor I wrote my dissertation titled "Machine Learning Assessment of Novel Biomarkers for Aggressive Prostate Cancer," which earned a grade of 95% and won me the prize of "Best Dissertation in Computer Science 2024".

Through my years I have developed skills in Python, ML, R, and Git, and I'm continually expanding my knowledge in ML, as well as medicine, through both academic and personal projects. My goal is to use these skills to innovate within the healthcare industry, using ML to advance medical research and improve patient outcomes.
